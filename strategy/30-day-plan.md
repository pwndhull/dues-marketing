# Dues — Initial 30-Day Marketing Plan

**Budget ceiling:** AUD $100/month. Organic-first. No paid spend without explicit approval.  
**Principle:** establish a trustworthy baseline before setting numeric growth targets.

## Week 1 — Establish the baseline
- Verify current App Store listing, product claims, subscription options, screenshots, privacy policy, and supported locales.
- Export a suitable historical period from App Store Connect and subscription analytics.
- Keep raw exports local in `analytics/imports/`; produce a reviewed aggregate baseline report.
- Audit listing clarity, screenshot sequence, keyword coverage, and obvious conversion friction.
- Deliver: baseline report, evidence-backed ASO audit, and prioritised task list.

## Week 2 — Prepare ASO improvements
- Research relevant search phrases and competing listings; record source URLs and access dates.
- Draft title/subtitle/keyword-field and description changes only where applicable to current Apple fields and limits.
- Draft screenshot storyboards using real product screens and verified feature claims.
- Deliver: listing drafts, keyword map, screenshot brief, and measurement plan.
- Do not submit changes without user approval.

## Week 3 — Organic content and one controlled test
- Draft a small batch of helpful short-form content about organising recurring bills and remembering due dates.
- Use actual app UI where appropriate; avoid invented user stories and unsupported claims.
- Choose one measurable experiment based on baseline evidence (for example, one listing-message hypothesis or one content format).
- Define success metric, comparison, duration, and decision rule before launch.
- Public posting or listing changes require explicit approval.

## Week 4 — Review and decide
- Compare results with the pre-test baseline and account for sample size, seasonality, and attribution limitations.
- Document what worked, what did not, and what remains inconclusive.
- Update the next 30-day plan based on evidence.
- Only propose paid tests if there is a clear hypothesis, estimated spend, stop condition, and user approval.

## Metrics to collect if available
- impressions, product-page views, first-time downloads, conversion rate;
- territory and source/channel breakdown;
- proceeds, active subscriptions, trials, conversions, cancellations, and refunds where available;
- content reach and clicks where platform analytics provide them.

Do not set a numeric target until a verified baseline exists. Treat any future target as a hypothesis, not a guarantee.

**Status:** Draft; not approved for posting

## Script
**Hook:** “Start with a list—not a complicated system.”

**Steps:**
- Write down recurring household bills.
- Add the next known due date.
- Mark anything that needs checking because the amount or date can change.
- Review the list weekly.

**Close:** “Keep it simple enough that you’ll actually use it.”
